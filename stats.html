    How the score is built

    • 100 points for a cleared mission
    • −12 points for every attempt after the first, never below 40
    • +20 points for a solve under 30 seconds, +10 under a minute
    • −15 points for every hint revealed
    • ★★★ first attempt with no hints · ★★ up to three attempts and one hint · ★ otherwise
